About AICSM

Making practical education and skills more accessible

All India Computer Saksharta Mission (AICSM) is an educational organization run under the governance of RJITS & AICSM. Established in 1999, it focuses on delivering IT and technical computer education at accessible fees to students across India.

AICSM's training-center model is intended for education providers interested in offering structured learning opportunities in computer education and other skill-based fields.
